What is Agentic AI?

AI agents autonomously perform multi-step tasks, make decisions, and use tools, going far beyond simple automation. They act, learn, and adapt in real time within your sales processes.

Workshop Goal

Leave with a clear, prioritised shortlist of 3 to 5 agentic AI use cases specific to KUKA's sales context, with business rationale and a path to implementation.

Why Now?

Enterprise AI agents are reaching production-readiness. Early movers in B2B manufacturing sales are capturing significant efficiency gains and competitive advantage.

Rate Sales Domains

For each of KUKA's six sales domains, discuss and rate how much potential you see for Agentic AI. This helps us prioritise where to go deeper.

Guiding Question

"Where in our sales organisation do you feel the most time is lost to manual, repetitive, or slow processes today?"

Consider: Where do your sales reps spend time that could be automated? Where does speed matter most? Where is data underutilised?

Think about daily frustrations Data-heavy tasks Where speed = competitive advantage Repetitive approvals or searches

Rate each domain on AI-Automation Potential

1 = Low potential today  |  3 = Medium  |  5 = High potential, strong case for AI agents

Select Focus Areas

Drill into each domain and select the specific focus areas where you see the highest potential for AI agents. Aim for 5 to 10 areas total across all domains.

Guiding Questions

"Which of these specific activities, if supercharged with AI, would have the biggest impact on KUKA's sales performance?"

Ask: Where is information scattered or hard to find? Where do decisions take too long? What would a smart assistant that works 24/7 unlock for your team?

Information overload? Slow decision cycles? Manual communications? Compliance overhead?
